HELLO, I'M DASINDU

Creator at heart.
Founder by action.

I'm Dasindu Sanujana, a web creator and technology enthusiast from Sri Lanka, and the founder of MCT Corporation (Micro Central Technology).

I'm interested in how design and technology work together — from building a website that feels effortless to shaping a digital product that solves a real problem. I value clarity, curiosity and learning by building.

MCT is where I bring those interests together, exploring digital services, applications and new ideas. My approach is hands-on: learn the tools, experiment with the possibilities, then turn the most useful ideas into something people can actually use.
